Welcome to my cybersecurity portfolio and technical blog.

This platform documents my professional development in Information Security, combining academic background, corporate experience, and hands-on technical exploration.

## 🔍 About Me

I'm Allan Vitor, graduated in Information Management from the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE), and currently pursuing a degree in Information Security, further strengthening my technical foundation in cybersecurity.

I have professional experience in corporate security environments, having completed internships at Dell Technologies, where I worked with cybersecurity operations, access management, governance routines, and security processes.

## 🗂️ Blog Structure

This site is structured into the following sections:

- **Home** - Latest posts and updates
- **Categories** - Organized content by topic
- **Certificates** - My most relevant certifications
- **Tags** - Tags allow you to access all posts related to a specific topic.
- **Archives** - Posts are organized in chronological order by their publication date.
- **About** - More about me and the purpose of this blog

## 🧩 Purpose of This Blog

- Document technical studies and experiments
- Share structured security insights
- Track certifications and continuous learning
- Build a professional footprint in cybersecurity
