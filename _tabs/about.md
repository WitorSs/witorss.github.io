## Hello, I'm Allan Vitor

I'm a cybersecurity practitioner with a developer's background, focused on
**offensive security**. I graduated in Information Management from the Federal
University of Pernambuco (UFPE) and I'm currently pursuing a second degree in
Information Security to deepen the technical foundation behind the hands-on
work.

I interned at **Oracle Brazil** in a corporate security environment, where I
worked with security operations, access management and governance routines. That
experience taught me how security works inside a real organization, not just in
a lab.

What sets me apart is that I don't only *use* security tools, I *build* them.
Coming from development, I write my own tooling to automate the repetitive parts
of the work, and I document the reasoning behind each one. You can see two of
them on the [Tools](/tools/) page: a Nmap report generator and an SSH log
analyzer, each with a full write-up.

### What I focus on

- **Offensive security**: penetration testing, CTF labs, and exploitation
  fundamentals. Most of the write-ups here come from hands-on TryHackMe rooms.
- **Security tooling**: building small, focused tools in Python that solve a
  real problem and explain the thinking behind them.
- **Solid fundamentals**: a working understanding of the defensive side too,
  because you can't attack what you don't understand.

### About this site

This is where I turn study into practice. I share technical write-ups, the tools
I build, and the certifications I earn along the way. Each post is meant to show
not just *what* I did, but *why*, the decisions and the reasoning, which is the
part that actually transfers to the job.
